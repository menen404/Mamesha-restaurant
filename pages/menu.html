Our Food and Drink

Enjoy variety of delicious traditional and cultural dishes at our restaurant. More specifically, we serve Gurage's cultural food.

Food Description
Kitfo Traditional Ethiopian dish made from minced beef(It could be from Ox,sheep or goat meat)
Sega Wet A beef stew cooked with traditional spices and onions.
Gomen Besega Collard greens cooked with meat and with little bit of butter.
Tikul Another version of "Kitfo" but this one is boiled.
